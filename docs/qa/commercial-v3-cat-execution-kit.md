# Independent acceptance of the commercial architecture (CAT-01 to CAT-40): execution kit

Prepared 2026-11-13 from `docs/traceability/acceptance-tests.csv` (ZID-COM-WF-2026-11 v3.0, section 15). It is a procedure for Independent QA. It approves nothing and passes nothing: gate G5 needs CAT-01 to CAT-40 executed with evidence by people who did not build the system.

## What counts as independent

- The tester did not write or review the engineering test for the row they execute.
- The tester observes the behaviour themselves (the procedure column) in the environment named in the evidence record. Reading the engineering test, or re-running it, is useful context and is not execution.
- Every row is recorded as PASS, FAIL or BLOCKED with the evidence below. A row that depends on a value the owners have not signed (a price book, a seller entity, a threshold) is BLOCKED with that reason, not passed on a fixture.
- Nothing here is marked executed in the repository. The register's IN_PROGRESS status means only that engineering evidence exists.

## Before starting

1. A non-production environment with the commercial migrations applied (head `commercial_0037` or later) and the signed or sandbox policy files the row names.
2. At least one ACTIVE market matrix for a test market (four independent signers, none the author), with its rows taken through the rollout to CANARY. Matrix rows are references: use test references and label them as such.
3. Seeded principals for: a company Owner, a Billing Admin, a Developer Admin, a Member, an Admin, Support, a Super Admin, and the finance roles the finance policy names. Record who plays each.
4. Engineering's evidence tests can be run as context: `cd backend/app && python -m pytest <path>::<test>` with `ZID_TEST_PG_URL` set to a disposable PostgreSQL for the component tests.

## Evidence to keep for every row

Environment and build identifier, date and time (UTC), tester name, the principals used, the steps as performed, the observed result, screenshots or API transcripts with identifiers visible, and the verdict. Store them with the row id in the release evidence register. A failure opens a defect that names the row.

## Rows that cannot be passed on engineering's word

- CAT-10, 22, 23, 24, 25, 39 depend on the authority thresholds and discount bands, which are proposals until the Founder, CFO and CCO sign. Execute them against the signed values, or mark BLOCKED.
- CAT-01 to 05 and 34 to 35 depend on real per-country evidence in the matrix. A row filled with test references proves the rule, not the market.
- CAT-38 needs real devices and a screen reader. The automated checks in the repository (axe in a simulated browser) are structural evidence only.
