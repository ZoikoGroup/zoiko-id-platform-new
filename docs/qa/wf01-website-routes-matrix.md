# Identity verification without the chip: which routes the website can complete

Prepared 18 Nov 2026 (CR-114) from the code, not from the wireframes. "Complete" means: built, tested, and able to produce its stated outcome once its owners approve it. Nothing here is approved; the market policy file ships empty, so no route can produce a result until an owner adds a market.

| Route | Where it lives | Website status | What it can honestly conclude | Still needed before it is live |

## What this means for a launch with no app

The only route that can produce a result on the website alone is the reviewed photograph. It is a limited level. Say so in the product wording and in any customer contract: "reviewed by people, document not cryptographically verified".

## Update 18 Nov 2026 (CR-125)

What was open on the reviewed route and is now built, with its limits:

- **An approval counts downstream.** An approved case leaves an `IDENTITY_VERIFIED_FOR_SCOPE` claim with its grade (`REVIEWED_NO_CHIP` for this route) and a stop date; screening and the company grade read it. Whether they should accept that grade is the owners' decision, and nothing filters it yet.
- **Rights requests find, export and retire these cases.** **Uploads are scanned and re-encoded without metadata** (proved in the real photo service). **A reviewer queue with a deadline.** **A reviewer can attest the date** where the platform could not read it. **No printed data is kept** on this route.
- **Still not done:** a real photograph, reviewer, browser or phone has not been through it (the script is `wf03-reviewed-route-uat.md`); the retention, validity and deadline periods are unset (owners); the DPIA, lawful basis and notice are drafts; reviewers are untrained; the retention sweep is not proved on these photographs in a deployed cell; one architecture exception (the photograph is held by the evidence module, against the letter of VERIFY-22) needs ratifying.
