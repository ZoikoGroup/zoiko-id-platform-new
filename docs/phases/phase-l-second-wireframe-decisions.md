> **Source:** the 22 distinct documents in `zoikoid_2ndwireframes` (issued 28 Oct 2026, all **proposed, awaiting founder approval**). Document 11 is an unsigned ratification form whose default posture is NO-GO. These are local-only .docx files, like the other specs.

Back to the [phase index](README.md).

## How this phase works

The documents are worked **one at a time, in dependency order**. A slice is closed only when its document's own acceptance tests pass, each linked in `docs/traceability/acceptance-tests.csv`. Every slice follows the same five steps:

1. **Gap check**: compare the document with the code that already exists, and list what is built, what conflicts and what is missing.
2. **Decide**: record any choice the document leaves open, and raise a change request where it conflicts with an earlier spec.
3. **Build**: backend first, then the contract (OpenAPI, events, action catalogue), then the console screen.
4. **Prove**: the document's acceptance tests, including its negative cases, as automated tests.
5. **Record**: status table below, traceability links, and the owner actions that engineering cannot close.

## Principles every slice must keep (they repeat across all 22 documents)

1. **Identity, authority and evidence are separate facts.** A registry match, an ID check, a paid plan or a login never implies authority to act for a company.
2. **Fail closed.** A provider timeout, an unsupported country, missing data or a stale proof is never "verified" and never "approved".
3. **The server decides.** Roles, plans, countries, step-up and approvals are enforced at the API, job and webhook, never only in the UI.
4. **Payment, plan and role do not buy trust.** Baseline security, audit, sanctions and dual approval cannot be switched off by any tier.
5. **Unratified numbers stay disabled.** Money thresholds and approver names are configuration that is inactive until a signed policy version exists.

The per-document designs, taken only from the documents, are in [phase-l-design.md](phase-l-design.md).

## Work that engineering cannot close

These stay open for the named owners, and no slice may mark them done:

- Founder approval of every document (all are proposed).
- Signed discount, commitment, credit and pilot thresholds, and named internal approvers (AE, CCO, CFO, Founder, Finance roles).
- Supplier contracts, pricing, coverage proofs and data-processing terms for Veriff, Sumsub, Creditsafe, D&B and sanctions content.
- The six-approval country activation record (legal, supplier, privacy, security, support, sign-off) for each country.
- The Zoiko iD Assured Review service (staffing and operational approval) used by one-person organizations.
- Any claim of certification (NIST levels, UK trust framework, regional data centres).
