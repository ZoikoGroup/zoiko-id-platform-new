# Applicant notice (draft): checking your identity with a photograph, in your browser

**Status: DRAFT for counsel and the DPO. Not approved, not versioned, not for use. The bracketed parts are decisions this draft does not make.** It is written to be read by the person being checked, in plain words, before the photograph is taken.

---

## Checking your identity with a photograph of your document

**What we are asking you to do.** Take a photograph of the page of your passport or identity card that has your photo and the two lines of letters at the bottom. People at [company name] will look at it and decide whether to accept it.

**Why.** [The reason, in the company's words: for example, because it must check who you are before opening your account.] [Lawful basis, as counsel decides.]

**What we do with the photograph.**
- We check that it is clear enough to read. A computer measures things like sharpness and glare. It does not decide whether your document is genuine, and it does not decide anything about you.
- We remove hidden information from the file, such as where and on what device it was taken, before we keep it.
- We keep the photograph **encrypted** for [N] days, so that two people at [company name] can look at it. After that we delete it.
- We do not keep the details printed on your document in any other form.

**Who can look at it.** Only people at [company name] who are allowed to review identity checks, and only when they give a reason. Each time is recorded. They cannot be you. Nobody else sees it.

**What can happen next.**
- Two reviewers accept it, and your identity check is complete [for N days].
- A reviewer asks for a clearer photograph. That does not count against you.
- A reviewer cannot accept the document. You can contact [contact] or start again with another document.

**This is not the strongest kind of check.** Reading the chip inside your passport with the phone app is stronger, because it proves the document's data was signed by the country that issued it. If you have the app and a phone that can read your chip, you can choose that instead.

**Your choices and rights.**
- You may choose another way to verify [list the alternatives the market allows].
- You may ask to see what we hold, to have it deleted, or to withdraw, by contacting [contact]. [Where deletion is limited by law, say so here.]
- You may complain to [supervisory authority].

**Who we are.** [Controller and contact details, as counsel decides.]
