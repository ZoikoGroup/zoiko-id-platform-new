# Person-first sign-up: what only the owners can close (ZID-UX-WF-013A; slice L-11, CR-048)

1. **Founder approval** of the 13A wireframe and its acceptance tests (the document is proposed).
2. **Seed and configure the registration tenant per cell:** run `python -m zid_platform.entrypoints.seed_registration_tenant --apply`, then set `ZID_REGISTRATION_TENANT_ID`. Person-first sign-up is off without it.
3. **Country registry:** a person can sign up only where the country capability registry has approved REGISTRATION for the deployed environment (today GB is a sandbox, so a deployed environment answers "not available yet" with the contact route until the six approvals in `country-capability-owner-pack.md` are recorded). Legal and Privacy also confirm the contact route shown to people in closed countries (default `support@zoikogroup.com`).
4. **Country table** (`backend/config/registration/country-routing.yaml`): ruled GB only on 2026-11-02. Legal and Privacy confirm that, and decide when each EU or U.S. row is switched back on (it needs its cell to pass the readiness gates first).
4. **Verification mail:** enable template ZID-EM-001 (and later 002 and 003), route `single_use_verification_url` to the web verify page, and approve the sender identity and copy. Without a route and an enabled template, no mail is sent.
5. **Notice and legal copy.** The privacy notice page now also describes what a personal account stores (country, the address until it is proved and then only a one-way fingerprint, journey, language, notice version, the single-use link as a hash, the passkey's public key; no documents, photo, phone, proof of address or card; 30-day deletion of ended requests; removal on erasure). It is engineering's statement of what the platform does, for Privacy and Legal to approve or reword, and its version is now **2026-11-03** in the page, the cell's `ZID_TENANT_SIGNUP_NOTICE_VERSION` default and the tests; a cell that pins the old value in its own environment must change it, or sign-up asks people to read the current notice. **The Terms of Service do not exist yet:** Legal must supply them. Until then the sign-up page shows no link to them and `/legal/terms` is "not found"; publishing is one switch in `frontend/apps/web/src/lib/legal.ts` plus the text in the terms page.
6. **Anti-abuse proportionality.** Built: a signed, time-limited nonce, an in-process limit per client that stays bounded in memory and sees the real client only when the ingress is trusted (`ZID_FORWARDED_ALLOW_IPS`), a per-address hourly cap in the database, and the mail estate's flood policy. Security sets the edge limits for `/v1/registration/*` (the production control) and decides any further bot control; it must not exclude assistive technology. The nonce is not single-use; say if it must be.
7. **SSO providers:** ruled none for now (2026-11-02). Approving Microsoft or Google later needs real provider apps, contracts and a policy for what an SSO email claim may prove.
8. **Alternative to a passkey: engineering decision to ratify.** The only fallback is the governed recovery path (independent approval, re-proofing), never an email or text code, because anything weaker than the passkey would be the way in for an attacker who controls an inbox. No other authenticator type is built for sign-up. Security and Product confirm that, and approve the wording and assurance policy of the recovery path.
9. **Held requests.** Ruled read-only lookup (2026-11-02). Support wording and the rule that nothing may be said about a hold or an account are drafted in `docs/runbooks/signup-operations.md` section 3 for Security to confirm. There is still no release procedure; until the owners decide one, a person starts again when the old request ends.
10. **Retention.** Ruled 30 days after a request ends (2026-11-02). Built since: an erased account takes its account index, requests and invitation routes with it (CR-048), and only that erasure can delete them. Privacy confirms the lawful basis for the keyed address hash while an account lives.
11. **Browser, screen-reader and device evidence** for AC-10 (320 px, landscape, 200 percent zoom, announced errors), run by QA.
12. **Threat model.** Drafted in `docs/threat-model/signup.md` (14 threats, each with its control and the test that proves it, and the residual risks); Security, Privacy and QA adopt or amend it.

13. **Running a cell.** `docs/runbooks/signup-operations.md` lists what an operator sets and checks: the registration tenant on the cell and the worker, DELETE for the worker role on the three registration tables, the trusted ingress addresses, mail settings and the edge limits. Confirm the worker role grants and who owns each step.
