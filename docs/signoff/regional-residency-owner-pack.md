# Owner pack: ZID-EDR-002 regional residency

This is a checklist for the owners. It approves nothing and every reference below is blank on purpose: a gate is passed only when an owner records a real reference in `backend/config/cells/cell-readiness.yaml`. The loader refuses `status: READY` unless all five gates have a reference, and until then a cell holds the Regional offer in every deployed environment (`CELL_NOT_READY`).

Source: ZID-EDR-002 sections 2, 5 and 8 (proposed, awaiting founder approval). Phase: [L-2](../phases/phase-l-second-wireframe-decisions.md).

Facts engineering cannot supply:

- **us-1 and us-1-dr** are catalogued targets in `backend/config/cells/cells.yaml`. The AWS regions there (`us-east-1`, `us-west-2`) are proposed. SRE must confirm or replace them and declare the real location of every subsystem before the `architecture` gate can be recorded.
- **uk-1** has no approved recovery cell. EDR-002 asks for "U.K.-bound recovery"; one has to be named, approved and declared before `uk-1` can pass.
- **eu-1** recovers to `eu-1-dr` (Ireland, same EEA zone). Confirm that is the approved EEA recovery.

## 3. Sales controls

Customer wording is fixed in `backend/config/residency-offers/residency-offers.yaml`. A lint test fails the build if the product or its configuration says "global data residency", "data never leaves your country" or "100% sovereign". Sovereign and Dedicated are enterprise quotations only; no price or service level is set by EDR-002.
