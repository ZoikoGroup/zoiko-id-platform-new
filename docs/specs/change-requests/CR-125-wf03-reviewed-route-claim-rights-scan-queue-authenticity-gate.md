# CR-125: the reviewed route's remaining gaps: the claim an approval leaves, rights requests, scanning and cleaning, the queue, date attestation, and the authenticity evidence gate

Source: Wireframe 03 (ZID-DTV-WF-03) alternate route, T24 and T25; WF01 section on the web-only path. Builds on CR-124. Status: engineering built 18 Nov 2026. Not run with a real photograph, reviewer or browser.

## What was built

- **An approval counts downstream** (`application/idv_claims.py`, migration `verification_0025`, `verify.idv_case_claim`): an APPROVED case under a market that sets `result_valid_days` leaves one claim, `IDENTITY_VERIFIED_FOR_SCOPE`, carrying its **grade** (`CHIP_VERIFIED` only when the chip's data was proved issuer-signed, otherwise `REVIEWED_NO_CHIP`), the market, the pinned rules and the date it stops counting. `current_identity_claim` (what screening rests on) and `person_assurance_current` (what the company grade reads) now read it beside the verification-session assertions, newest first. A market with no validity period leaves **no claim**, and the case says `NO_CLAIM_ISSUED:RESULT_VALIDITY_NOT_SET`. This is also the validity period and the re-verification: the claim lapses and the person runs a new case.
- **Rights requests cover these cases** (`verification/infrastructure/privacy_adapter.py`, store `verify.idv_case`): a person's cases are found; the export says what was decided and why (codes), whether a photograph is held and until when, and the claim it left, never the image; disposal withdraws the claim and retires the photograph (its bytes are destroyed with the subject's key by the erasure job). The closed case row holds no document data.
- **Nothing of the printed data is kept on a route with no chip** (no salted digest of name, number or birth date): there is nothing to compare it with.
- **Uploads are scanned and cleaned** (`api/idv_cases.py`, the photo service's new `/sanitize`): scanned for malware first (a deployed cell with no scanner refuses with 503 rather than pretend to scan; an infected file is refused with 422); then re-encoded by the photo service as a plain JPEG with orientation applied, every piece of metadata (location, device, time) removed, transparency made white and the longest side capped. The checks, the printed-line reading and anything kept use the clean copy. If the service cannot clean it, nothing is read or kept. Proved against the **real container**: `tools/ocr-sidecar/smoke_in_container.py` (12 checks, all passing) with real Pillow and Tesseract.
- **A reviewer queue with a deadline** (`GET /v1/idv/review-queue`, a card on the reviewer screen): oldest first, never the reviewer's own case, not one they already signed, with how long each has waited and, where the market sets `review_sla_hours`, when it falls due and whether it is overdue.
- **A reviewer can attest the date** (`POST .../attest-date`) only when the platform's own reading could not establish it (a document with no machine-readable lines, or lines that did not read reliably): logged as the reviewer's act and recorded as an attestation, never as the platform's reading, and once a date is established nobody changes it.
- **The authenticity evidence gate** (`domain/authenticity_evaluation.py`, `config/idv/authenticity-gate.yaml`, `tools/scripts/authenticity_eval.py`): the rule for what labelled results a template, tamper or photo-substitution check must show before it may be switched on for a document type. Synthetic samples never count; an attack not clearly failed counts as accepted; every named attack kind and enough phones are needed; each document type and check is judged alone. It **ships with no policy**, so it never passes, and **no authenticity check is produced**: this is the gate, not the check.
- **Documents**: `docs/privacy/dpia-addendum-idv-reviewed-route-DRAFT.md`, `docs/privacy/idv-reviewed-route-notice-DRAFT.md` (drafts for the DPO and counsel), `docs/qa/wf03-reviewed-route-uat.md` (the script for the real browser and phone runs), `docs/qa/photo-service-licence-sheet.md` (facts from the installed image for whoever signs the register), and the market policy file now documents `media_retention_days`, `result_valid_days` and `review_sla_hours`.
- Tests: `test_idv_gaps_pg.py` (claim read by screening and the company grade, no claim without validity, scan, a service that cannot clean, no printed digest, date attestation, the queue, the rights adapter), `test_authenticity_evaluation.py`, `test_authenticity_tools.py`, the earlier media tests, and the reviewer-screen logic tests.

## An architecture exception that needs the owners' ratification

ZID-VERIFY-001 VERIFY-22 (pinned by `test_raw_evidence_access.py`) says the verification module never stores or reads raw identity documents: normalised results and commitments only, with the raw material staying with a provider. The reviewed route has no provider and no chip, so for two reviewers to decide, the platform has to hold the photograph. Rather than loosen the rule, the custody is **in the evidence module** (`evidence/application/review_photo.py`, which owns the vault), the verification module reaches it only through that port and only from `idv_media.py`, opening a photograph uses the **existing** protected raw-evidence read act (`evidence.object.read`, R3 and E3) on top of the reviewer's reason and grant, and the guard now pins that single exception (and that nothing else in verification touches the vault or the port). This is a deliberate deviation from the stated principle for one route. The architecture owner should ratify it or reject the route.

## Not built, not proved, not decided

- **Whether screening and the company grade should accept the `REVIEWED_NO_CHIP` grade is a decision for the owners.** Both now read the claim as they read a session assertion, and the grade travels with it so a stricter consumer can filter; nothing filters yet.
- **No real photograph, reviewer, browser or phone** has been through any of this. The UAT script is how it is done.
- **The retention sweep removing these photographs is not proved** in a deployed cell, and no retention period, validity period or deadline is set anywhere: they are the owners' decisions and ship unset.
- **Printed-document authenticity is still not built.** The gate defines the evidence; genuine specimens and attack samples do not exist here.
- **The DPIA, lawful basis and notice are drafts**, unsigned. Reviewers are untrained and have no stated appeal route for an applicant.
- **Documents with no machine-readable lines** can now be dated by a reviewer's attestation, but the quality checks and the printed-line reading still assume a passport-style page.
- **Selfie and face match in the browser** remain parked: no engine approved.
- The photo service is running on this machine but is not deployed anywhere, `ZID_OCR_URL` is still empty by default, and its licences are read in part only (`photo-service-licence-sheet.md`).
