# CR-110: WF03 signal model, one deterministic decision, and the controls around it

Source: Wireframe 03, ePassport NFC, document authenticity and biometric proofing (ZID-DTV-WF-03). Status: engineering for the decision rules and controls built 17 Nov 2026; nothing in this change reads a chip, runs a liveness engine or names an assurance level.

## What was built

- `verification/domain/idv_decision.py`: the signals as separate state sets (optical, NFC access, passive authentication, chip or active authentication, face match, liveness) and `decide(signals, policy)`, which returns APPROVED, REVIEW_REQUIRED, REJECTED, INDETERMINATE or ABANDONED with every reason, the claims it supports and the policy version it was pinned to. Rules: reading a chip is not a verified chip; no trust root gives CERT_MISSING and INDETERMINATE, never a pass and never an accusation; only a broken signature, a manipulated document or an expired document under a REFUSE policy rejects; a web capture never claims the chip; clone resistance is its own claim and unsupported is never a pass; a failed or inconclusive presentation check is never repaired by a good face score; a face outcome never rejects on its own; printed text or a typed transcription alone never approves; printed data differing from the chip, failed MRZ check digits and capture-injection risk block automatic approval; expiry follows the market policy; an alternative route exists only if the signed policy names it, carries no chip-grade claim and needs distinct reviewers; a lost entitlement or unreleased method makes the route INDETERMINATE.
- `verification/domain/idv_controls.py`: biometrics are OFF until a lawful basis, an impact assessment and a notice are recorded, the current notice was shown, consent (where it is the basis) is given and not withdrawn, and a self-hosted engine has an independent presentation-attack evaluation; trust material is accepted only from an allowlisted authority per country and kind; a trust change names the cases it touches; retry ceilings per market stop an attempt and an unset ceiling allows none.

## Wired to a stored case (added the same day)

- **Evidence into signals** (`idv_evidence.py`): the platform's own passive-authentication verdict becomes the WF03 chip signals (no revocation check gives REVOCATION_UNKNOWN, never a pass; a missing root gives CERT_MISSING); the capture envelope is checked against a server-issued one-time nonce bound to the case, freshness, a media digest seen before (a replay), a virtual-camera flag and a device-attestation flag, and what cannot be established is "not established", never clean; the printed-document check results become PASS, FAIL, REVIEW or UNKNOWN (a fatal check that failed is manipulation, other failures and doubt ask for a person); printed holder data is compared with the chip's DG1 after a documented normalisation.
- **Market rules file** (`idv_policy.py`, `config/idv/market-policies.yaml`): read strictly, hashed, and shipped with no market, so a case for an unlisted market is refused and nothing runs on a default. A malformed entry refuses the whole file; an unknown key is an error.
- **Stored case** (migration `verification_0020`: `verify.idv_case`, `verify.idv_case_decision`, `verify.idv_media_hash`, all row-level secured): a case is pinned to the market's rules by digest, its decision is re-derived and stored on every change with an append-only history row, APPROVED, REJECTED and ABANDONED close it (a trigger refuses any change afterwards), and nothing is deleted. Retry ceilings per market are enforced; biometric steps are refused unless the market's basis, impact assessment, notice, consent and (for a self-hosted engine) independent evaluation are recorded, and withdrawing consent switches the person signals back off.
- **API** (`/v1/idv/cases`): open, list, read with history, nonce, nfc-state, chip, capture, optical, consent, consent withdrawal, abandon and a reviewer's signature (`verification.idv.review`, a protected action). No route accepts a result: signals come from the platform's checks. Only the applicant adds evidence; a reviewer is never the applicant and signs once.
- **Webhook** `com.zoiko.id.verification.idv.case_decided.v1`, raised when the decision changes, carrying codes, states and the policy digest, never document data, an image or a score.
- **Screen**: the console page "Identity proofing cases" shows each decision with its reasons in plain words, the six signals, the claims that may be repeated, the policy digest, and the reviewer signature.

## How it is proved

`test_idv_decision` (17), `test_idv_controls` (6), `test_idv_evidence` (9), `test_idv_policy` (3) and, on PostgreSQL, `test_idv_case_pg` (3: the full approve-and-close path, replay, nonce reuse, missing trust, retry ceilings, consent withdrawal, route rules, reviewer rules, tenant isolation, append-only history). The architecture guards pass with the new modules.

## Not built

- **No optical document-security engine exists.** The printed-document checks (template, tamper, photo substitution, fonts) take an engine's results as input; nothing in this repository inspects an image, so the checks must be supplied by an engine that has been evaluated.
- **No device or app attestation is wired.** The envelope carries the platform's attestation result when there is one; without it the capture is "not established" and the market decides whether that blocks approval.
- **Active authentication is not accepted on this route** (the chip route records it as not tested); the older self-run route still performs it.
- **The face engine is not called from the case routes.** `record_engine_result` is the one place a liveness and face result is accepted and is refused unless the biometric controls are in force; no route calls it until an engine is approved.
- **A reviewer's signature does not change a chip-route decision.** It only matters on a signed alternative route; a missing trust root can never be turned into a pass by a person.
- **Route enablement is not tied to provider entitlements yet.** `decide` returns INDETERMINATE when a route is disabled, but the case flow always passes it as enabled.
- Applicant screens P01 to P07 need a native or mobile build and a real phone with a chip; the console shows cases, it does not capture.
- No liveness or face engine was evaluated; no presentation-attack lab result exists; no impact assessment, lawful-basis decision, notice text or trust-list authority list is recorded. Those are people's decisions (D01 to D10) and are not faked.
