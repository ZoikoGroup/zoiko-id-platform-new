# CR-045: Capability bands C1 to C5 and usage reservations (ZID-ENG-DEC-008), slice L-8

Status: open (built). The decision record is proposed and awaits founder approval. It also says the Commercial Estate, not the record, stays authoritative for plan names, prices, quantities, trial rules and SLAs, so the Estate keeps deciding until the owners rule.

## What changes
- **Configuration `config/entitlements/capability-bands.yaml`**: the record's two feature tables (40 features) as C1 to C5 cells, generated from the record by script. `bands` maps C1 to C5 to DEVELOPER, LAUNCH, BUSINESS, SCALE, ENTERPRISE (an engineering default by order; Commercial & Finance map them to signed SKUs). `band_matrix_enforcement` is `REPORT_ONLY` (default) or `ENFORCE`. Each feature names the Estate capability that carries it (`catalogue`), an engineering mapping for the owners to confirm.
- **Configuration `config/entitlements/meter-register.yaml`**: the eight meters (auth.mau, idv.check, kyb.check, credential.issue, credential.verify, api.request, agent.principal, data.retention) with what each counts and its guardrail. `priced: false` and `hard_quota: false` everywhere: the record approves no number.
- **Domain `commercial/domain/bands.py`**: load-time invariants (the eight baseline rows are mandatory in every band and nothing else is; a higher band keeps what a lower one includes; five bands map to five different plans). `decide_feature` is the entitlement step of the record's order of operations. `reconcile` lists where the Estate and the record differ. `downgrade_impact` discloses what a move down switches off.
- **Resolver `commercial/application/entitlement_resolver.py`** and `commercial.ports.require_feature`: Estate gate first, then the band matrix, then the commercial enforcement mode. Shadow and off report a commercial refusal without blocking it; region, missing provider route and unknown facts are never shadowed. An unreadable entitlement refuses in enforce mode and holds in the others.
- **Routes:** `GET /v1/entitlements/effective`, `GET /v1/entitlements/downgrade-impact?to=C1..C5`, `GET /v1/usage/summary`, all under `commercial.read`. `require_feature` is also called where a paid feature is created: enterprise SSO connection, SCIM source, agent registration, custom domain.
- **Migration `commercial_0015`, table `commercial.usage_reservation`**: one row per (tenant, idempotency key); RESERVED moves once to COMMITTED or RELEASED; terms never change; nothing is deleted (database trigger). `usage_reservations.reserve`, `commit`, `release` are idempotent, serialized per meter and period under an advisory lock, and refuse only the crossing operation with `QUOTA_REACHED` (HTTP 429: limit, used, reserved, reset date). A hard-quota meter with no readable limit answers `QUOTA_UNDEFINED` (503, a manual commercial decision). Only `auth.mau` can have a cap, from the plan's included active humans, and only when its register entry sets `hard_quota`. The reservation ledger is the quota ledger of this API; committing writes no invoice line and creates no price.
- **Report:** `python -m zid_platform.entrypoints.entitlement_report` prints every Estate and record difference from the live catalogue (read only).

## Completion pass
- **Routes.** `POST /v1/usage/reservations`, `POST /v1/usage/reservations/{id}:commit` and `:release`, on a client with the `usage:write` scope (the platform's own services and workers; a signed-in person without the scope is refused 403 `SCOPE_INSUFFICIENT`). Idempotent by key; a replay answers with `replayed: true`.
- **Events** (catalogued in `proposed-events.yaml`, emitted through the outbox, ids and quantities only): `commercial.usage.reserved`, `commercial.usage.committed`, `commercial.usage.released`, `commercial.quota.threshold_reached`. A reservation's events share one aggregate, so they carry versions 1 (reserved), 2 (quota reached) and 3 (committed or released). Quota reached is raised when a reservation brings the use exactly to the cap; a refusal raises none (it rolls back). The record also lists `contract.activated`, `plan.changed`, `entitlement.revoked`, `override.expired` and `service.suspended`: plan and contract changes are the existing `commercial.entitlement.changed` and order events, and the others have no source yet, so they are not added. `commercial.entitlement.snapshot_published` is added (see the shared snapshot below).
- **Billing bridge.** `bill_on_commit` on a meter in `meter-register.yaml` (default false). When true and the meter has an Estate meter (`auth.mau`, `credential.issue`, `credential.verify`), committing records one billable event keyed by the reservation, so a retried commit records nothing twice. A released reservation never reaches the ledger. Off until the owners rule that this API, not the operation itself, is the recording point.
- **Signed snapshot cache** (`entitlement_cache.py`). A snapshot is the tenant's strict decision for all 40 features, signed with the evidence runtime's internal MAC key. It is reused only while unexpired (`snapshot_ttl_seconds`, default 60, bounded 1 to 300, or the earliest live override expiry if sooner), correctly signed, and its fingerprint equals the tenant's commercial state now. The fingerprint is one read of the active entitlement, every override including revocations, and the billing state, so a plan change, suspension, billing restriction or override revoke ends it on the next call. Roles, sessions and tokens are never cached: they are decided fresh by the role matrix. Region and provider coverage are layered on per request. The cached answer equals the uncached answer for every feature and fact (tested exhaustively).
- **Residency.** `decide_in_region` takes the residency module's `authorize_transfer` as a handed-in gate (commercial cannot import tenant: the dependency-cycle test refuses it). The gate's answer is the `region_ok` fact; an unreadable residency record is a HOLD. An Enterprise tenant with a contracted feature is still denied a transfer the residency rules do not allow, and allowed once a rule is approved.
- **A defect found and fixed:** a region or coverage refusal for a feature the plan lacks carried an `upgrade_band`, which would have shown a paid upgrade as a way past a compliance block. Compliance reasons now never carry one.
- **Web:** a "Plan features" tab on the Billing page (the five-band view with the band that would unlock each feature, never offered for a region or route hold; a downgrade comparison that says it deletes nothing; this period's meters with how incomplete and duplicate checks are treated). Types and endpoints are in the API client.

## Shared snapshot and plan-aware sharing
- **Migration `commercial_0016`, table `commercial.entitlement_snapshot`:** one row per tenant with the latest signed snapshot, tenant-isolated. A process that has nothing in memory reads this row and uses it only if it has not expired, verifies under its own MAC key and its fingerprint equals the tenant's commercial state now; otherwise it builds a new one and replaces the row. So a second process, a restart or a scaled-out replica does not rebuild what is already current, and a row signed under another key is refused.
- **`commercial.entitlement.snapshot_published`** is emitted when the fingerprint changes (a plan, suspension, billing or override change), with `revision` as its version. A plain refresh after the lifetime saves the row quietly and announces nothing. Publishers of one tenant are serialized under an advisory lock, and a snapshot that is already out of date when it reaches the lock is not published, so a slow process cannot put an older state back. A failure to publish never fails a decision.
- **`plan_aware_transfer_gate`** (`commercial/application/transfer_gate.py`) wraps the residency gate: residency answers first and a refusal stands whatever the plan; if residency allows, `decide_in_region` asks whether the plan includes the feature that sends the data, and a refusal becomes `ENTITLEMENT_REQUIRED` (or `ENTITLEMENT_HOLD`) with the field allowlist withdrawn. It is the gate shared identity uses (`shared_assertions`, composed in `main._plan_aware_sharing`), for the feature `standard.credential-verification-validation`. In shadow mode a commercial refusal is reported and does not block.

## Known limits
- **No billable-ledger bridge by default** (`bill_on_commit` is off) and no price, cap or overage is created by reservations.
- **Not every listed event exists** (see the completion pass).
- **EDR08-09** covers the entitlement snapshot only. Role revocation and compromised tokens are decided fresh by the role matrix and sign-in, so there is no cached grant to invalidate; each process still checks the fingerprint itself on every decision, so a stale copy in memory or in the shared row is never used.
- **Only shared identity moves data between cells on a plan check today,** and its feature (`standard.credential-verification-validation`) is my reading of which band feature that sharing belongs to. Any later cross-cell route composes the same gate.
- **Feature to capability mapping** (for example "delegation workflow templates" to `consent_receipts`) is mine and is listed for the owners.
- **The snapshot TTL (60 seconds) is an engineering default;** the record asks for a bounded policy value and approves none.
