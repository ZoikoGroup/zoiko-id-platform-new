# CR-109: Company verification grades, two-reviewer approval, document age rules and market policy packs (ZID-DTV-WF-02 K03, K04, K07, K09; builds on CR-043, CR-101)

Status: **built on the engineering owner's instruction of 2026-11-17; sign-offs UNSIGNED.** Pure rules and one wired change. Nothing here switches a market on: the packs are default-off and the policy decisions D-01 to D-10 are people's.

## Why

Wireframe 02 asks for registry-first company verification with a governed document fallback. Most of the estate already exists (company verification cases CR-043, registry-backed roots, ownership chains CR-101, proof documents). What it lacked was the part that stops a document upload being shown as a verified company: named grades with honest labels, two independent reviewers for approvals no authoritative source backs, per-class document age rules, and market packs that cannot be enabled or published without their controls.

## What was built

- **Grades K0 to K4** (`organization/domain/kyb_grade.py`). A grade describes which claims are proven; it is not a privilege. Two reviewers on current documents give K1 "Documents reviewed" (never "verified company"); an exact, active, fresh register match gives K2; K3 adds a complete ownership assessment, verified people and clear screening where required; K4 adds a verified person whose mandate is in force. A company the source reports dissolved is K0. A missing, stale or unavailable source lowers the grade and is never a finding that the company does not exist. `permitted_actions` is computed at the time of the act: a binding act needs K4, a named verified person, a power the mandate lists and, where the mandate requires it, the second signatory. Recheck is due at 180 days (K1) and 365 days (K2) at most, sooner by policy, and immediately on a status change, dissolution, ownership change or dispute, mandate revocation, sanctions alert or fraud signal.
- **Two reviewers** (`organization/domain/dual_review.py`, wired into `kyb.review`). For a case with no authoritative record, the first approval is recorded as a first finding and the case stays in review; a second reviewer who is not the first, and not the requester, completes it; a disagreement escalates to Compliance and is never settled by the last writer; an escalated case needs the new protected action `organization.kyb.escalation.decide` and a third person. A rejection needs one reviewer (it protects). Position markers are restated by each step and never inherited. A case a register did return keeps the single-reviewer path.
- **Document age rules** (`organization/domain/company_documents.py`). Each document class proves named claims, each with its own age rule: an incorporation certificate proves formation and does not age, a registry extract speaks for current status and goes stale, a mandate counts inside its own dates until revoked. An old certificate is never rejected for age and never stands in for a current-status proof; a quarantined file proves nothing. An unmet claim is a request for evidence, never a rejection.
- **Market policy packs** (`organization/domain/market_policy.py`). A pack lists problems by code until it is complete: no evidence route, a source with no lawful basis, credential or recorded terms, fewer than two reviewers for document-first approval, an ownership threshold that is missing or unsigned (no universal percentage), a missing retention schedule for any record class, no legal hold or deletion job, recheck periods past the provisional ceilings without ratification. Publishing needs the pack's tests passed, the four sign-off roles (Compliance, Legal, Privacy, Security) from people other than the author, approvals bound to the exact pack digest, and two different approvers for a high-impact change. Activation adds an effective interval and rewrites nothing, so past decisions replay against the pack that made them; a rollback adds a new interval.

## Stored grade, mandates, ownership edges and the stored policy (added later the same day)

- **The grade is stored and read live** (`organization_0010`, `kyb_assessment.py`, `kyb_company.py`). Each assessment is an append-only row with the grade, the claims, why it is not higher and the plain facts it was worked out from (ids and codes only). `GET /v1/kyb/assertions` returns the grade and what is allowed NOW, worked out again from the stored facts at the time of the question, so a mandate that has ended or a register check that has gone stale lowers it with no one editing a row. `organization.kyb.assertion_issued` and `organization.kyb.assertion_revoked` go out when claims are gained or lost. Facts are gathered from what exists: the latest verification case (register and reviewers), the latest ownership graph and the decisions on it, screening, and the mandates. A beneficial owner counts as a verified person only when the decision confirming them points at a current identity assertion for exactly that person (`verification:<principal id>`). Identity and screening are supplied by the composition root (`configure_sources`): organization imports neither module, and until they are wired nobody is verified and screening is NOT_RUN.
- **The grade is read at the moment of an act** (`protected.py`, `config/kyb/action-gate.yaml`). A catalogued action that is a binding act (`payment.release`, `mandate.create`) is checked against the company's standing when it happens. The gate is OFF by default (`ZID_KYB_GATE=off`: nothing is read), `shadow` reports without refusing, `enforce` refuses with `KYB_GRADE_INSUFFICIENT`. A company with no assessment is K0 with nothing allowed, so `enforce` is switched on per market only after the companies in it have been assessed.
- **Mandates** (`organization_0011`, `kyb_mandate.py`). The representative never records their own; evidence is required; the acts are the binding ones only; payments, ownership transfer and delegation always need a second signatory, who is neither the representative nor the person who recorded it (the tables repeat these rules); revocation is a new fact with a reason and the row stays. `POST /v1/kyb/mandates`, `:cosign`, `:revoke` under `organization.kyb.mandate.manage`; each reassesses the company.
- **Ownership edge types** (`ownership.py`, additive: every stored graph keeps its meaning). VOTING links give a candidate by votes whatever the capital share; a NOMINEE link attributes the share to the named beneficiary, or is a gap when none is named; a TRUST or ARRANGEMENT with no person recorded as controlling it is a gap; links may carry the dates they were in force and are left out for a date outside them; a DISPUTED link makes whoever depends on it NEEDS_EVIDENCE and is a gap; shares in force at any one moment cannot add up to more than the whole, counted apart for capital and votes; a cycle through any share-bearing link is refused whole.
- **The stored market policy** (`organization_0012`, `kyb_market_policy.py`). Versions are immutable and identified by their digest; each sign-off is bound to the exact digest and its author cannot give it; publishing refuses with every missing control named; activation closes the market's open interval and opens a new one; rollback is a new interval with a reason; `effective_at` answers which pack was in force at a moment so a decision replays against the pack that made it. `organization.kyb.policy_activated` goes out on each change. The pack's own checks are the completeness rules: a pack that fails them has not passed its tests.

## Appeals, rechecks, export, signed extracts and the screens (added last)

- **Appeals** (`organization_0013`, `appeal.py`, `kyb_operations.py`): the applicant's side opens one against one assessment version inside the window, with a reason; one open at a time; it is decided UPHELD or ALLOWED by someone who made no part of the decision and is neither the applicant nor whoever opened it (the table repeats the last rule). An allowed appeal adds a new assessment; nothing earlier is edited. The applicant sees a safe message that names no threshold, rule or detector. Routes `GET /v1/kyb/appeals`, `POST /v1/kyb/appeals`, `POST /v1/kyb/appeals/{id}:decide` under `organization.kyb.appeal.open` and `organization.kyb.appeal.decide`.
- **Rechecks**: `recheck_pass` is a worker step (`company_grade_recheck`, after the company refresh) that reassesses each company whose next check is due; `apply_event` reassesses at once on an event that can invalidate a grade (a registry status change, dissolution, an ownership change or dispute, mandate revoked or expired, a representative removed, a sanctions alert, a fraud signal). One failing company never stops another.
- **Webhooks**: `organization.kyb.assertion_issued` and `organization.kyb.assertion_revoked` are subscribable through the existing delivery, which is already signed, idempotent, versioned and tenant-scoped. They carry grades, claims and reason codes only.
- **Export** (`POST /v1/kyb/evidence-exports`, `organization.kyb.export`): a signed summary of the grade history, watermarked with who asked and why, with no company name, address or number and no personal data.
- **Signed extracts and QR codes** (`extract_authenticity.py`, with the signature check in `infrastructure/extract_verifier.py`): an extract is the issuer's word only if a key from the trust list verifies it with an allowed algorithm inside the key's validity and not revoked; a QR counts only if its destination is, exactly, a host the issuer is recorded as using (https, no credentials in the address, no IP, no look-alike, no unlisted subdomain) AND what it returns matches the extract by digest. Anything else is NOT_PROVEN and goes to a person; an issuer that cannot be reached is never a finding against the file. This is a rule and a verifier: no registry's trust list is loaded here, and fetching the QR destination is the adapter's job.
- **Retention, erasure and legal holds**: the platform already runs these through the evidence vault and the privacy module (legal holds, `erase_subject`, disposal by retention class, PARTIAL outcomes). `retention.py` adds the KYB part: the honest answer to an erasure request (what goes, what stays and why, when backups clear), and a deletion plan that never deletes a class with no schedule. The pack's retention days are not yet wired into proof storage, which still takes its period from `proof-policy.yaml` (null, meaning kept until the owners decide): which purpose and risk tier a given company's proofs belong to is an owner decision.
- **Company verification screen** (`/admin/company-verification`, K06 and K08): the grade and what it means, why it is not higher and what can move it up (in words, never a rule or a threshold), what is allowed now, history, mandates (record, second signature, revoke), appeals, and the signed summary. **Reviewer position** (K07): the operator ruling screen now says where a case stands after each ruling (waiting for a second reviewer, escalated, approved by two, decided by Compliance). Identifier checks by market (K01: UK number format, a U.S. file number only with its state, a German number with its court, names kept in their own script) were already in place and are now pinned by tests.
- **Outages**: every way a register can fail (refused, throttled, broken, slow, redirected, too large, not JSON, no credential) is SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE, with a test for each.

## The operator route and screen for market packs, and the last gaps (added at the end)

- **K09 operator plane**: six new operator capabilities (`kyb.policy.author`, one approval per sign-off role `kyb.policy.approve_compliance|legal|privacy|security`, `kyb.policy.publish`) and a separation-of-duties rule: whoever proposes a pack approves or publishes none of it, each approver signs in one role only, and whoever publishes signs nothing. Routes under `/v1/kyb/market-policies` (list, one version with everything still stopping it, propose, `:approve`, `:publish`, `:rollback`), each through the operator gate with one capability, the change and its protected audit record in one transaction, and a refusal audited afterwards. The audit carries ids, codes and digests, never the pack. The operator console has a page, "Company verification packs" (Regulatory): versions, what is incomplete in words, who has signed and who is still needed, sign, publish, propose from JSON, roll back.
- **Concurrency**: assessments of one company queue on a transaction-scoped lock, so a recheck, an event and a person's request that coincide take distinct versions (tested with eight in parallel).
- **Issuer trust list and QR fetch**: `config/kyb/issuer-trust.yaml` ships empty, so nothing is trusted until Legal and Security record each issuer's keys and exact QR hosts; a malformed entry refuses the whole list. `qr_fetch` requests only an address the issuer is recorded as using, over https, following no redirect, with a timeout and size ceiling, and any failure is "could not be reached", never a finding about the file. `extract_check.check` joins the signature and QR paths and answers NOT_PROVEN for anything else.
- **Appeal outcome notice**: a decided appeal raises an alert in the applicant's own inbox with the safe message, once.
- **Retention wired**: the published pack's period for raw documents now sets how long a proof is kept in the vault (`proof.add_proof`), falling back to the proof policy's own setting when no pack is published for the market. Which purpose and risk tier a company's documents are filed under is read from `config/kyb/market-defaults.yaml`, marked PROPOSED until the owners decide.

## What it does not do

- **No load or regional-failover run has been done.** Every way a register can fail has a test, and parallel assessments are safe, but no sustained-load or failover exercise was run against a deployed cell.
- The issuer trust list ships empty (nothing is trusted until people record real keys and hosts); the retention purpose and risk tier are a proposal until the owners decide; an appeal outcome is an in-app alert, not an email.
- It does not connect any registry. UK Companies House, GLEIF and a gated French INPI adapter are the existing adapters' business; no source is claimed connected here.
- The grade assessor and the packs are rules, not yet a stored object or an API: there is no `GET .../assertions`, no `kyb.*` event and no customer screen for grades in this change.
- Escalation uses one new protected action; whether a Compliance role holds it is an owner decision. Until someone does, an escalated case waits.
- Not built: the QR and signed-registry-extract authenticity check, the ownership-graph edge types beyond CR-101 (voting, nominee, trust), the representative mandate service, retention and erasure jobs, the K01 to K10 screens, and every test that needs a real source or a real reviewer population.
