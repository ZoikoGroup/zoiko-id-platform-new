# RB-07: Outbox or queue lag

**Services:** signals, provisioning, evidence · **Tier:** T1 · **Owner:** @zoiko/sre

## Trigger

`outbox-priority-lag` invariant; domain-lane age above 60 s; consumer lag growth.

## Impact

Downstream projections, webhooks and revocation propagation fall behind.

## Preconditions

Pausing producers needs `platform.cell.manage`.

## Diagnosis

1. Check relay health and broker availability; separate priority from domain lanes.

## Mitigation

1. Prioritize the priority lane; scale consumers; pause bulk producers if required. Committed outbox rows survive a broker outage (SRE-12).

## Recovery

1. Drain idempotently; consumers dedupe by event id (inbox).

## Validation

1. Lane ages back under budget; no duplicate side effects (inbox check).

## Rollback

Resume paused producers.

## Escalation

SEV1 when the priority lane breaches the R3 budget (RB-03).

## Evidence

Relay positions before and after.
