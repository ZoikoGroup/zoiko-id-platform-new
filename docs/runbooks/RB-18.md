# RB-18: Bad release rollback and change freeze

**Services:** all · **Tier:** T1 · **Owner:** @zoiko/sre

## Trigger

Canary health gate failure; burn-rate PAGE after a deploy; exhausted error budget.

## Impact

Customer-visible regression until rolled back.

## Preconditions

Rollback to the last known-good digest needs no new build (promotion by digest).

## Diagnosis

1. Correlate the regression with `release.deployed`; check schema expand/contract stage.

## Mitigation

1. Roll back to the previous digest; freeze changes for the service (`POST /ops/services/{id}:freeze-changes`).

## Recovery

1. Fix forward through the normal pipeline; unfreeze with approval.

## Validation

1. Burn rate back under 1.0; canary gates green on the fixed release.

## Rollback

Unfreeze (`:unfreeze-changes`) once the error budget policy allows.

## Escalation

SEV2; SEV1 if a T0 journey regressed.

## Evidence

Release manifest of both digests; freeze and unfreeze records.
