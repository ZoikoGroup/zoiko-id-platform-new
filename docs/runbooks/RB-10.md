# RB-10: Object-store degradation (evidence and backups)

**Services:** evidence, backups · **Tier:** T1 · **Owner:** @zoiko/evidence

## Trigger

Object-store write errors or latency; evidence vault append failures.

## Impact

Evidence and backup durability at risk. Acknowledged E2/E3 evidence has a zero loss budget.

## Preconditions

None for diagnosis; switching paths needs `platform.cell.manage`.

## Diagnosis

1. Separate read and write failures; confirm whether acknowledgements were sent.

## Mitigation

1. Queue or fail critical durability paths rather than acknowledge without storage.

## Recovery

1. Flush the queued evidence in order; verify the chain.

## Validation

1. Evidence checkpoint verification passes; no acknowledged record missing.

## Rollback

Return to the primary storage path.

## Escalation

SEV0 if any acknowledged E2/E3 evidence is lost.

## Evidence

Checkpoint verification output attached to the incident.
