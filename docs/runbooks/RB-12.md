# RB-12: Cell disaster failover

**Services:** all cell services · **Tier:** T0 · **Owner:** @zoiko/sre

## Trigger

Cell or region declared lost; the in-region failover (RB-04) cannot restore a writer.

## Impact

Tenants homed in the cell are unavailable until promoted in their approved recovery cell. A controlled DENY beats split-brain.

## Preconditions

ACTIVE `platform.dr.execute` JIT grant (R3, two approvers or break-glass with review). Recovery cell readiness reported.

## Diagnosis

1. Confirm the failure is regional, not a dependency (RB-04, RB-11).
2. `GET /platform/recovery-readiness?cells=...` for the recovery cells.

## Mitigation

1. `POST /platform/tenants/{id}:failover` with the approved recovery cell.
2. Advance in order: FREEZE_OLD_WRITER, ASSESS, PROMOTE_DATA, ACTIVATE_KEYS, ADVANCE_EPOCHS, VALIDATE, ROUTE, RECONCILE (`POST /platform/operations/{id}:advance`). The platform refuses any other order.

## Recovery

1. Quarantine the failed cell (`/platform/cells/{id}:quarantine`); open reservations are RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED, reconciled by the mandate owners.

## Validation

1. VALIDATE probes all pass; the old writer epoch is refused everywhere (`WRITER_EPOCH_STALE`); discovery returns the new cell.

## Rollback

No abort after ADVANCE_EPOCHS. Return only through a governed failback (RB-13).

## Escalation

SEV0/SEV1 by scope; Security Lead and Privacy for residency-relevant moves.

## Evidence

The operation's append-only step log, gate reports and E3 audit records; record a DR exercise for real events too.
