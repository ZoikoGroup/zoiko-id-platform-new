# RB-13: Governed tenant migration or failback

**Services:** all cell services · **Tier:** T1 · **Owner:** @zoiko/sre

## Trigger

Planned rebalancing, residency change, or return after a failover.

## Impact

Brief write quiesce for the tenant during cutover.

## Preconditions

ACTIVE `platform.migration.execute` (migration) or `platform.dr.execute` (failback) grant; destination residency approved.

## Diagnosis

1. Destination readiness and capacity; residency profile and prohibited cells.

## Mitigation

1. Migration: PREPARE, COPY_VERIFIED, QUIESCE, ADVANCE_EPOCHS, VALIDATE, ROUTE, RETIRE_OLD_COPY.
2. Failback: PREPARE, RESYNC_VERIFIED, FENCE_RECOVERY_WRITER, ADVANCE_EPOCHS, VALIDATE, ROUTE.

## Recovery

1. Retain the old copy for the rollback window only, then erase it.

## Validation

1. Canary sign-in, decision and evidence probes for the tenant; placement epoch advanced once.

## Rollback

Before cutover: abort. After cutover: a new governed migration back.

## Escalation

SEV2 if a migration stalls during quiesce beyond 15 minutes.

## Evidence

Step log, verification hashes as evidence references.
