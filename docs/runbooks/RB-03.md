# RB-03: Revocation or security-signal propagation breach

**Services:** signals, authorization · **Tier:** T0 · **Owner:** @zoiko/security

## Trigger

`revocation-propagation` invariant (R3 p99 above 2 s in-cell); synthetic revocation probe (OPS-13) failing.

## Impact

A revoked credential, session or grant may be honoured beyond its propagation budget: a security exposure, not only an availability one.

## Preconditions

Operator Console access at AAL-Z3. Pausing producers needs `platform.cell.manage`.

## Diagnosis

1. Check priority-lane outbox age (`zid_outbox_oldest_unpublished_seconds{lane="priority"}`) and relay health.
2. Check consumer lag for the security-state and session projections.
3. Check whether bulk producers (SCIM imports, mapping releases) are saturating shared resources.

## Mitigation

1. Pause bulk producers; the priority lane always drains first (OPS-15).
2. Raise the PEP freshness requirement so R3 actions fail closed until propagation recovers.

## Recovery

1. Scale relay and consumers; drain the backlog idempotently; resume bulk producers.

## Validation

1. Synthetic revocation observed by every enforcing consumer within 2 s p99 for 30 minutes.

## Rollback

Lower the temporary PEP freshness override back to policy defaults.

## Escalation

SEV1 immediately; Security Lead when any R3 action may have been allowed after revocation.

## Evidence

List the affected window; DecisionReceipts in that window are reviewed for decisions made on stale state.
