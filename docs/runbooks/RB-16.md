# RB-16: Signing key compromise or emergency rotation

**Services:** authentication, verification, federation · **Tier:** T0 · **Owner:** @zoiko/security

## Trigger

Suspected key misuse; unexpected signatures; HSM audit anomaly.

## Impact

Tokens or credentials signed with the key cannot be trusted.

## Preconditions

Security Lead declares; two-person key ceremony.

## Diagnosis

1. Identify key id, purpose and the time window of exposure.

## Mitigation

1. Publish a new key; stop signing with the old one; shorten token lifetimes.

## Recovery

1. Revoke the old key from JWKS after the overlap window; re-issue affected credentials.

## Validation

1. Relying parties fetch the new JWKS; old kid rejected.

## Rollback

Not applicable.

## Escalation

SEV0; legal and customer communications per the communications plan.

## Evidence

Key ceremony record; affected token/credential ids list.
