# RB-14: Backup restore and point-in-time recovery

**Services:** all cell services · **Tier:** T1 · **Owner:** @zoiko/sre

## Trigger

Data corruption, operator error, or a restore exercise.

## Impact

Restores go to an isolated environment first; production is not overwritten in place.

## Preconditions

ACTIVE `platform.migration.execute` grant. Crypto-erased subjects must stay erased (SRE-19).

## Diagnosis

1. Choose the recovery point; confirm keys for that point are available.

## Mitigation

1. `POST /platform/restore-jobs`; steps SELECT_POINT, RESTORE_ISOLATED, VERIFY_INTEGRITY, PROBES.

## Recovery

1. Promote corrected data through a governed migration or targeted repair, never a blind overwrite.

## Validation

1. VERIFY_INTEGRITY probes: database consistency, evidence chain, erasure preserved.

## Rollback

Discard the isolated environment.

## Escalation

SEV1 when restore is needed for production data.

## Evidence

Observed RPO recorded as a DR exercise result.
