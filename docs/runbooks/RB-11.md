# RB-11: KMS or HSM throttling or outage

**Services:** authentication, verification, evidence · **Tier:** T0 · **Owner:** @zoiko/security

## Trigger

KMS throttling errors; signing latency; recovery key readiness false.

## Impact

Signing and envelope encryption slow or fail. There is no plaintext or static-key fallback (SRE-23).

## Preconditions

Recovery key path changes are part of a declared failover (RB-12).

## Diagnosis

1. Identify which key purposes are throttled; check quotas and region health.

## Mitigation

1. Throttle non-critical crypto (credential issuance, bulk re-wrap) first; use approved capacity failover only.

## Recovery

1. Restore quota or region; resume deferred crypto work.

## Validation

1. Signing latency normal; no artifact was signed or stored unencrypted.

## Rollback

Re-enable deferred work.

## Escalation

SEV1; SEV0 on suspected key compromise (RB-16).

## Evidence

Key-management audit records cover every key operation.
