# RB-05: Replica lag

**Services:** all cell services · **Tier:** T1 · **Owner:** @zoiko/sre

## Trigger

Replica lag above 5 s (T1) or 30 s (T2) for 5 minutes; `cell_readiness.replica_lag_s` breaching the DR RPO.

## Impact

Reads that need R2/R3 freshness fall back to the writer; DR recovery point degrades (failover ASSESS will block).

## Preconditions

Read-only diagnosis; throttling jobs needs `platform.cell.manage`.

## Diagnosis

1. Identify the workload: long transactions, vacuum, bulk imports, IO saturation.

## Mitigation

1. Throttle non-critical reads and batch jobs; never route R2/R3 reads to a lagging replica.

## Recovery

1. Resolve the IO cause; let the replica catch up; report readiness again (`PUT /platform/cells/{id}/readiness`).

## Validation

1. Lag under target for 30 minutes; ASSESS gate passes for the recovery cell.

## Rollback

Re-enable throttled jobs.

## Escalation

SEV2; SEV1 if the DR replica stays beyond RPO during a declared disaster.

## Evidence

Readiness reports are retained by the resilience module.
