# RB-09: Cache or projection outage

**Services:** authorization, tenant routing · **Tier:** T0 · **Owner:** @zoiko/authorization

## Trigger

SpiceDB/projection unavailable; routing cache errors.

## Impact

Decisions needing relationships cannot be proven; they fail closed.

## Preconditions

None for diagnosis.

## Diagnosis

1. Check projection health and its last applied relation revision.

## Mitigation

1. Bypass to the authoritative store where the risk class allows; never let a stale cache create authority.

## Recovery

1. Rebuild the projection from the authoritative relationship log; verify revision parity.

## Validation

1. Projection revision equals the authoritative revision; synthetic ALLOW/DENY pass.

## Rollback

Remove the bypass once parity is proven.

## Escalation

SEV1 if T0 decisions are failing closed for more than 5 minutes.

## Evidence

Revision numbers before and after the rebuild.
